01

Check Responsiveness & Call 911

Sec 0 - 10

Tap victim firmly on both shoulders and shout loudly: “Are you okay?”. Look for normal chest rise or breathing. If unresponsive, shout to a specific bystander to dial 911 and retrieve an AED immediately.

warning Agonal gasping is NOT normal breathing. Initiate CPR immediately.
02

Push Hard & Fast (Center of Chest)

Depth: 2.0 - 2.4 IN

Interlock fingers with hands stacked flat. Place heel of bottom hand on the center of the chest (lower half of breastbone). Lock elbows directly straight. Position shoulders perpendicular to chest. Push downwards at least 2 to 2.4 inches (5-6 cm) and allow complete chest recoil between pumps.

straighten Recoil: Complete relaxation without lifting hands
TARGET: 24 COMPRESSIONS / 12 SEC
03

Continuous Cycles Until Relieved

No Interruption

Do not stop compressions unless victim clearly shows signs of movement or consciousness, a certified EMS team takes over, or an Automated External Defibrillator (AED) is active and analyzing heart rhythm.

cached Rotate compression operators every 2 minutes (approx. 200 cycles) to avoid exhaustion.
FIG 01: STERNUM PLACEMENT
Visual Alignment Check

Avoid ribs and upper stomach. Sternum heel alignment minimizes skeletal fracture risk and optimizes aortic blood circulation during cardiac standstill.
