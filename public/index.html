01

Check Responsiveness & Call 911

Tap victim firmly on both shoulders and shout loudly: "Are you okay?". If unresponsive, call 911 and get AED.

02

Push Hard & Fast (Center of Chest)

Interlock fingers, push at least 2-2.4 inches deep at 100-120 BPM. Allow complete chest recoil between pumps.

03

Continue Until Relieved

Don't stop until EMS arrives or victim shows signs of life. Rotate operators every 2 minutes.
